**How the numbers are made.** Each bucket is split at the same ratio
(stratified by difficulty): test gets `round(n × (1 − ratio))` models, at
least one, and at least one always stays in train. Which models go to test
is a seeded shuffle (seed 42), so the split is reproducible. Difficulty is
the PCA score (PC1+PC2, refit on the 5,264 unique models).

`make_benchmark_split.py`).
